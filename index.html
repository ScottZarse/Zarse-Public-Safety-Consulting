SEC 01 — Overview

Fire service credibility, executed technology change.

Positioned to support prime consulting firms as an SME on public safety technology engagements.

Zarse Public Safety Consulting provides technology infrastructure consulting exclusively for public safety agencies and the firms that serve them. The firm is built around a combination that's genuinely hard to find: deep operational fire service credibility, paired with direct, executed experience delivering technology change inside a large, complex municipal department.

That means CAD replacement isn't theoretical — it's a joint fire/police procurement worked from RFP through vendor vote. Identity architecture isn't a slide — it's a full requirements document written to retire shared-password service accounts across an entire department. Radio interoperability isn't a briefing — it's SOPs written and taught in person, across every shift, at every station.

Available to support prime consulting firms as a subject-matter expert on public safety technology engagements, and open to direct engagements with fire departments and public safety agencies.

"A firefighter who speaks IT. Not an IT consultant who speaks firefighter."
